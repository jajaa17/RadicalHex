RadicalHex

Save editor for Pokémon Radical Red 4.1

Open your battery save (.sav or .srm) or drop it here. Close the game in your emulator first, or it may overwrite your edits when it saves.

  • A backup is made every time you open or save a file.
  • Every save is checked before it is written. If anything looks wrong, nothing is written.
  • Your edits only touch the newest save slot, so the game's previous save stays intact.
